Our story

Three generations at one bench

Our workshop has stood in the same lane in Jodhpur since 1974. The tools have changed; the joints have not.

Timber

Kiln-dried timber

Moisture brought down to 8–10% before a single cut, so the frame will not move in the monsoon.

Cane

Hand-woven cane

Every panel is woven by hand on the frame itself — which is why it sits tight twenty years later.

Joinery

Mortise & tenon

No staples, no cam locks. Joints that are cut to fit, glued, and left to cure for two days.

About our beds

Every bed is built from kiln-dried sheesham, joined with mortise and tenon, and finished by hand with a natural hard wax oil. Storage variants use a gas-lift mechanism rated to 40,000 cycles, and the slats are solid timber — never ply.
